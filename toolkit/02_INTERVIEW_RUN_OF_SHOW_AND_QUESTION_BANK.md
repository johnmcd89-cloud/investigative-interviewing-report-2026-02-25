# Interview Run-of-Show and Question Bank

## A) Run-of-show (45–75 min)
1. Opening and role clarity (5 min)
2. Process and anti-retaliation statement (3 min)
3. Open narrative (10–20 min)
4. Structured probes (15–25 min)
5. Evidence checks and clarifications (10–15 min)
6. Closure and next steps (5 min)

## B) Opening script (adaptable)
“Thanks for meeting. I’m conducting a company fact-finding investigation. I’m here to understand what happened from your perspective. We prohibit retaliation for participating in an investigation. I may ask follow-up questions to clarify details. If anything is unclear, say so and I’ll rephrase.”

(If counsel-led, add role/privilege language approved by legal.)

## C) Question sequence
### 1) Open prompts
- “Please walk me through what happened, start to finish.”
- “What do you remember most clearly?”

### 2) Anchor prompts
- “What date/time range are you most confident about?”
- “Where were you physically/virtually at that point?”

### 3) Detail probes
- “What happened immediately before and after that event?”
- “Who else was present or notified?”

### 4) Verifiability probes
- “What records could independently verify this?”
- “Any message, ticket, calendar, or access data tied to this?”

### 5) Clarification prompts
- “Earlier you said X; now Y. Help me reconcile that.”
- “Is your uncertainty about timing, sequence, or substance?”

## D) Questions to avoid
- Assumptive guilt framing (“Why did you do it?” too early)
- Threat/promise framing (“Admit this and it’ll go easier”)
- Compound confusing prompts
- Repetitive leading prompts after denial

## E) Closure script
“Let me summarize what I heard. Please correct anything inaccurate. Is there anything important we didn’t cover? Is there any evidence or person we should review?”

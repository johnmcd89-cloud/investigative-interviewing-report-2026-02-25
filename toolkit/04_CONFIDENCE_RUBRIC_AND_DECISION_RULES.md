# Confidence Rubric and Decision Rules

## Finding categories
1. **Substantiated — High confidence**
   - Strong, independent convergence on core allegation elements.
2. **Substantiated — Moderate confidence**
   - Mostly convergent evidence; limited unresolved material gap.
3. **Inconclusive**
   - Material unresolved conflict or missing critical evidence.
4. **Unsubstantiated**
   - Evidence leans against allegation; uncertainty documented.

## Suggested confidence bands (customize)
- High: >0.80
- Moderate: 0.65–0.80
- Low: <0.65

## Rules
- Do not use demeanor alone to elevate confidence.
- Do not collapse “Inconclusive” into “Unsubstantiated.”
- Require explicit alternative-hypothesis check before final finding.

## Proportional action guidance
- High confidence + high severity: strongest corrective/disciplinary options.
- Moderate confidence: prefer proportional controls plus targeted remediation.
- Inconclusive: preserve, monitor, and re-open on new evidence.

## PEACE (Planning and Preparation, Engage and Explain, Account, Closure, Evaluation)
- **Origin:** Developed in England/Wales in the early 1990s after wrongful-conviction concerns and reforms under PACE; introduced as a national model in 1992. (https://doi.org/10.1007/s11292-014-9207-6, https://www.police.govt.nz/sites/default/files/publications/investigative-interviewing-literature-2005.pdf)
- **Core steps:** 5 stages: planning/preparation, engage/explain, account, closure, evaluation. (https://www.police.govt.nz/sites/default/files/publications/investigative-interviewing-witness-guide-011024.pdf)
- **Where used:** UK-origin model; adopted/adapted in New Zealand and other information-gathering jurisdictions (e.g., Norway via KREATIV). (https://www.police.govt.nz/about-us/publication/investigative-interviewing-police-manual-chapter, https://doi.org/10.1007/s11292-014-9207-6, https://rm.coe.int/1680909713)
- **Strengths:** Structured, non-coercive, account-focused interviewing with open questions and rapport; supports consistency and transparency. (https://doi.org/10.1007/s11292-014-9207-6, https://www.police.govt.nz/sites/default/files/publications/investigative-interviewing-witness-guide-011024.pdf)
- **Limitations/criticisms:** Training transfer and supervision quality can be weak if implementation is poor. (https://www.police.govt.nz/sites/default/files/publications/investigative-interviewing-literature-2005.pdf)
- **Legal-ethical implications:** Fits rights-based policing frameworks (fairness, legal advice, safeguards for vulnerable people, recording). (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/pace-code-c-2019/pace-code-c-2019-accessible, https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/police-and-criminal-evidence-act-1984-pace-and-accompanying-codes-of-practice)
- **Reliability / false-confession risk:** Best current comparative evidence favors information-gathering approaches as more “diagnostic” than accusatorial methods (higher true/false discrimination). (https://doi.org/10.1007/s11292-014-9207-6)

## Reid Technique
- **Origin:** U.S. confession-oriented model (Inbau/Reid lineage), commonly described as behavioral analysis interview plus confrontational interrogation phases (including a 9-step structure in many descriptions). (https://www.police.govt.nz/sites/default/files/publications/investigative-interviewing-literature-2005.pdf, https://doi.org/10.1007/s11292-014-9207-6)
- **Core steps:** Pre-interrogation credibility assessment, then guilt-presumptive confrontation/minimization-style interrogation tactics aimed at confession. (https://doi.org/10.1007/s11292-014-9207-6, https://www.psychologicalscience.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/White-Paper-Police-Induced-Confessions.pdf)
- **Where used:** Historically widespread in the U.S.; accusatorial methods are also reported in Canada and some Asian settings. (https://doi.org/10.1007/s11292-014-9207-6)
- **Strengths:** Can increase true confessions from guilty suspects. (https://doi.org/10.1007/s11292-014-9207-6)
- **Limitations/criticisms:** Heavy reliance on unreliable deception cues, psychological manipulation, and pressure tactics; substantial false-confession concern. (https://doi.org/10.1007/s11292-014-9207-6, https://www.psychologicalscience.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/White-Paper-Police-Induced-Confessions.pdf, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28485886/)
- **Legal-ethical implications:** Some tactics are considered unacceptable in stricter non-coercive jurisdictions; deception/minimization raises major due-process concerns. (https://www.police.govt.nz/sites/default/files/publications/investigative-interviewing-literature-2005.pdf, https://www.wcl.american.edu/impact/initiatives-programs/center/publications/documents/upload/mendez-principles.pdf)
- **Reliability / false-confession risk:** Meta-analytic evidence shows accusatorial methods increase both true and false confessions (false-confession increase is a key risk signal). (https://doi.org/10.1007/s11292-014-9207-6)

## Cognitive Interview (CI / Enhanced CI)
- **Origin:** Developed by Geiselman and Fisher; enhanced version later added rapport/communication framework. (https://rheg.psychologyresearch.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/MemonMeissnerFraser2010_MetaAnalysisReview.pdf)
- **Core steps:** Classic mnemonics include context reinstatement, report everything, change order, and change perspective; enhanced CI adds witness-centered rapport and open prompts. (https://rheg.psychologyresearch.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/MemonMeissnerFraser2010_MetaAnalysisReview.pdf)
- **Where used:** Primarily witness/victim interviewing, often embedded within broader PEACE-style interviewing systems. (https://www.police.govt.nz/sites/default/files/publications/investigative-interviewing-witness-guide-011024.pdf, https://rheg.psychologyresearch.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/MemonMeissnerFraser2010_MetaAnalysisReview.pdf)
- **Strengths:** Strong evidence for increased correct recall; updated reviews replicate this with only small error increases and no clear confabulation-rate increase. (https://doi.org/10.1080/10683169908414991, https://rheg.psychologyresearch.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/MemonMeissnerFraser2010_MetaAnalysisReview.pdf)
- **Limitations/criticisms:** Time/training intensive; some CI components are modified/omitted in certain populations/settings. (https://rheg.psychologyresearch.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/MemonMeissnerFraser2010_MetaAnalysisReview.pdf)
- **Legal-ethical implications:** Non-coercive and memory-focused, but requires interviewer skill to avoid suggestive contamination. (https://rheg.psychologyresearch.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/MemonMeissnerFraser2010_MetaAnalysisReview.pdf)
- **Reliability / false-confession risk:** This is a witness-memory method, not a confession-elicitation method; risk profile is mainly memory error/contamination rather than false confession. **Inference based on method purpose and study designs.**

## NICHD Protocol (child forensic interviewing)
- **Origin:** Developed by NICHD researchers to operationalize child-development and memory science in forensic interviews. (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18023872/, https://nij.ojp.gov/topics/articles/child-forensic-interviewing-best-practices)
- **Core steps:** Structured phases with rapport/ground rules, practice narrative, open-ended invitations, then focused prompts if needed, and closure. (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18023872/, https://nij.ojp.gov/topics/articles/child-forensic-interviewing-best-practices)
- **Where used:** Internationally adapted in multiple countries (e.g., North America, Europe, Israel, Japan, etc.). (https://research-repository.griffith.edu.au/items/70f686b3-99b3-4ec4-9f57-65bdf80abe60)
- **Strengths:** Meta-analytic evidence shows substantially more invitations/open prompts, fewer suggestive/option-posing prompts, and greater central-detail yield. (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25942285/)
- **Limitations/criticisms:** Effects may be weaker in some preschool samples; implementation quality and supervision matter. (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25942285/)
- **Legal-ethical implications:** Strong fit with child-protection and anti-suggestibility requirements; designed to reduce interviewer-induced distortion. (https://nij.ojp.gov/topics/articles/child-forensic-interviewing-best-practices)
- **Reliability / false-confession risk:** Primarily improves child witness evidence reliability; it is not a suspect-confession method. **Inference:** likely lowers contamination risk via reduced suggestive questioning, but direct “false confession” metrics are not the target outcome.

## KREATIV (Norway)
- **Origin:** Norwegian investigative interviewing framework developed from PEACE principles after major miscarriage-of-justice/false-confession concerns in Norway. (https://rm.coe.int/1680909713, https://openarchive.usn.no/usn-xmlui/bitstream/handle/11250/3015387/2021DiepeveenSuspects_POSTPRINT.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=9)
- **Core steps/components:** Values-based staged process (communication, rule of law, ethics/empathy, active awareness, trust/openness, information; science-anchored), including early rights communication and account-based interviewing. (https://openarchive.usn.no/usn-xmlui/bitstream/handle/11250/3015387/2021DiepeveenSuspects_POSTPRINT.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=9, https://rm.coe.int/1680909713)
- **Where used:** Embedded in Norwegian police education/training pathways; integrated into specialization and ongoing training. (https://rm.coe.int/1680909713)
- **Strengths:** Emphasizes open-mindedness, mitigation of confirmation bias/tunnel vision, and information quality. (https://rm.coe.int/16809420e3, https://rm.coe.int/1680909713)
- **Limitations/criticisms:** Method-specific quantitative outcome literature is thinner than for PEACE/Reid comparisons. **Inference from available review/governance sources.**
- **Legal-ethical implications:** Explicit rights/procedural safeguards orientation; prosecutorial instructions and human-rights framing are central. (https://rm.coe.int/1680909713, https://www.wcl.american.edu/impact/initiatives-programs/center/publications/documents/upload/mendez-principles.pdf)
- **Reliability / false-confession risk:** Institutional and review sources explicitly position KREATIV as reducing false-confession risk; direct causal meta-analysis remains limited. (https://rm.coe.int/16809420e3)

## Conversation Management
- **Origin:** Developed within UK-derived investigative interviewing practice for reluctant/uncooperative interviewees; later incorporated into Commonwealth police doctrine/training materials. (https://www.police.govt.nz/sites/default/files/publications/investigative-interviewing-literature-2005.pdf)
- **Core steps:** More interviewer-managed account development than free recall; structured topic control and clarification/challenge for reluctant subjects. (https://www.police.govt.nz/sites/default/files/publications/investigative-interviewing-literature-2005.pdf, https://www.police.govt.nz/sites/default/files/publications/investigative-interviewing-witness-guide-011024.pdf)
- **Where used:** Used as a model alongside free recall and enhanced CI (e.g., NZ witness interviewing). (https://www.police.govt.nz/sites/default/files/publications/investigative-interviewing-witness-guide-011024.pdf)
- **Strengths:** Practical for low-cooperation interviews where pure free narrative is insufficient. (https://www.police.govt.nz/sites/default/files/publications/investigative-interviewing-witness-guide-011024.pdf)
- **Limitations/criticisms:** Higher interviewer control can raise leading/confirmation-bias risks if poorly executed. **Inference from doctrine plus false-confession literature.** (https://www.police.govt.nz/sites/default/files/publications/investigative-interviewing-literature-2005.pdf, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28485886/)
- **Legal-ethical implications:** Should remain within PEACE-style safeguards and non-oppressive questioning standards. (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/pace-code-c-2019/pace-code-c-2019-accessible)
- **Reliability / false-confession risk:** No strong method-specific meta-analysis isolating CM; risk appears implementation-dependent. **Inference.**

## Phased Interview Model
- **Origin:** Broadly refers to stage-based interview structures; in modern policing this often maps to PEACE phases. (https://www.police.govt.nz/sites/default/files/publications/investigative-interviewing-witness-guide-011024.pdf)
- **Core steps:** Plan/prepare, engage/explain, account, closure, evaluation (phase progression with possible model shifts during account). (https://www.police.govt.nz/sites/default/files/publications/investigative-interviewing-witness-guide-011024.pdf)
- **Where used:** Common in witness/suspect policy frameworks in PEACE-influenced jurisdictions. (https://www.police.govt.nz/about-us/publication/investigative-interviewing-police-manual-chapter)
- **Strengths:** Clear structure for supervision, training, and post-interview evaluation. (https://www.police.govt.nz/sites/default/files/publications/investigative-interviewing-witness-guide-011024.pdf)
- **Limitations/criticisms:** Phase models can fail if governance/training is weak (e.g., NZ’s CIPEM controversy and subsequent reform work). (https://www.police.govt.nz/news/release/police-response-ipca-report-complex-investigation-phased-engagement-model-cipem)
- **Legal-ethical implications:** Phase discipline can support rights compliance and documentation, but does not replace substantive legality/ethics checks. (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/pace-code-c-2019/pace-code-c-2019-accessible)
- **Reliability / false-confession risk:** Evidence is stronger for the *content* of phases (information-gathering vs accusatorial tactics) than for “phasing” alone. **Inference.** (https://doi.org/10.1007/s11292-014-9207-6)

## Strategic Use of Evidence (SUE)
- **Origin:** Developed in Scandinavian deception/interview research (Hartwig/Granhag and colleagues). (https://doi.org/10.1016/B978-0-12-394433-7.00001-4, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16977348/)
- **Core steps:** Plan evidence use; withhold/disclose evidence strategically (often late/incremental), compare statements with known evidence, leverage inconsistencies diagnostically. (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16977348/, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25844517/)
- **Where used:** Research-to-practice training in policing and investigative interviewing communities. (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16977348/, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25844517/)
- **Strengths:** Improved deception-detection performance in trained interviewers and increased admissions in experiments. (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16977348/, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25844517/)
- **Limitations/criticisms:** Evidence base still heavily experimental/mock-crime; field generalizability and misuse risk depend on training/safeguards. (https://doi.org/10.1016/B978-0-12-394433-7.00001-4)
- **Legal-ethical implications:** Strategic disclosure must remain non-coercive and rights-compliant; should not become deceptive oppression. (https://www.wcl.american.edu/impact/initiatives-programs/center/publications/documents/upload/mendez-principles.pdf, https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/pace-code-c-2019/pace-code-c-2019-accessible)
- **Reliability / false-confession risk:** Promising for diagnosticity vs blunt accusatorial approaches, but direct false-confession outcome evidence is still limited. **Inference.**

## Rapport-based interviewing
- **Origin:** Emerged from information-gathering interviewing science and formalized in modern law-enforcement training programs. (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33734749/, https://www.wcl.american.edu/impact/initiatives-programs/center/publications/documents/upload/mendez-principles.pdf)
- **Core steps:** Build conversational/relational rapport, use open productive questions, active listening, trust-building, respectful challenge. (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33734749/)
- **Where used:** Law-enforcement suspect/witness contexts, including federal/state/local training settings. (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33734749/, https://www.unodc.org/unodc/en/justice-and-prison-reform/investigative-interviewing.html)
- **Strengths:** Training studies show increased rapport behaviors and higher cooperation/disclosure; child-forensic meta-analysis also supports rapport effects. (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33734749/, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33454643/)
- **Limitations/criticisms:** Rapport is not a magic tactic; outcomes depend on interviewer competence and integration with evidence strategy. **Inference from training literature.**
- **Legal-ethical implications:** Strong alignment with anti-coercive human-rights standards (Méndez Principles/UN interviewing guidance). (https://www.wcl.american.edu/impact/initiatives-programs/center/publications/documents/upload/mendez-principles.pdf, https://www.unodc.org/unodc/en/justice-and-prison-reform/investigative-interviewing.html)
- **Reliability / false-confession risk:** **Inference:** likely lower false-confession risk than coercive methods because pressure/deception is reduced; direct head-to-head false-confession RCTs focused solely on rapport remain limited. (https://doi.org/10.1007/s11292-014-9207-6, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28485886/)
